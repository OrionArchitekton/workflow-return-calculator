How the estimate works

This is a capacity estimate: it prices time freed at the loaded hourly rate. It does not assume a headcount reduction or new revenue.

Weekly time freed = runs × (current minutes − review minutes − exception rate × recovery minutes) ÷ 60. Annual labor value uses 52 weeks. First-year net value subtracts setup and 12 months of running costs.

Estimated break-even divides setup cost by monthly labor value freed after running costs. A workflow with zero or negative monthly savings has no break-even point under these assumptions.

Before you automate

  • Time a sample of real cases instead of relying on the best case.
  • Count quality checks, failures, and rework. A fast wrong answer costs more.
  • Name the owner who will monitor exceptions and maintain the workflow.
  • Run a small pilot, then replace these assumptions with measured results. Use the pilot guide.
